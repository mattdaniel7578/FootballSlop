Rules

  • Each week's games are split into three sections: Primetime, Early games (1:00 PM ET kickoffs plus any London game), and Late games (4:05/4:25 PM ET).
  • Pick every Primetime game against the spread.
  • Pick exactly one game from the Early section, and exactly one game from the Late section. Picking a different game within the same section replaces your pick for it.
  • Picks can be changed anytime before that game's kickoff. Once kickoff passes, the pick locks.
  • Spreads refresh automatically from DraftKings roughly every {{ odds_sync_minutes }} minutes, then freeze ahead of kickoff: Thursday games freeze the prior Wednesday at 4:00 PM ET, Sunday and Monday games freeze the prior Saturday at 1:00 PM ET, and any other game freezes 24 hours before its kickoff.
